# Business Insights

##Summary
This analysis reviews clean sales performance and separates rows that need data quality review before they are used in bussiness decisions

## Key Findings

### 1. Highest Revenue Category
Electronics generated the highest revenue with **Rp16,051,500**, contributing approximately **62.8%** of total revenue.

### 2. Highest Profit Margin
Stationery recorded the highest profit margin at **44.88%**, making it the most profitable product category relative to its revenue.

### 3. Best Performing Region
Surabaya achieved the highest revenue (**Rp19,935,300**) and profit (**Rp4,490,300**).

### 4. Best Sales Channel
The Online channel contributed the highest completed sales revenue at **Rp20,229,000**.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend
Revenue peaked in **March** and declined afterward, with **June** recording the lowest revenue.

## Recommendation

Use clean rows for the main dashboard, but keep the issue log visible. The business should review non-clean rows before using them for final reporting, especially if they affect revenue, profit, or order completion.
